## Context

As Shubham EPC expanded its permanent staff and distributed site workforce, managing attendance, leave approvals, employee documentation, and field allowances through generic third-party SaaS software became increasingly problematic and expensive.

Commercial HRMS platforms typically charge hefty per-user per-month licensing fees. Worse, their off-the-shelf workflows failed to match the realities of construction site operations, where employees frequently move between remote locations and work under non-standard shift patterns.

## The Problem

- **Recurring Software Costs:** Paying annual per-seat licenses for `{{METRIC: total workforce count}}` employees, even though field workers only needed basic attendance and leave logging.
- **Workflow Mismatch:** Off-the-shelf software forced the operations team into rigid approval hierarchies that did not reflect actual project reporting structures.
- **Data Silos:** Extracting attendance and payroll records to match internal project costing sheets required manual Excel exports every billing cycle.

## What I Did

I initiated and led the development of **Shubham ONE**, a tailored internal HRMS designed specifically for the company's operating workflows:

1. **Strategic Scope Definition:** Stripped out enterprise bloat. Focused strictly on core high-friction modules: attendance tracking with geofencing, leave workflows, employee lifecycle document repository, and project allocation.
2. **Modern Architecture:** Built a fast, responsive web application using Next.js, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL that works seamlessly on mobile devices used by field staff.
3. **Role-Based Permissions & Site Hierarchies:** Designed access control that allows site managers to approve site-specific attendance while maintaining central governance for head office HR and accounts.
4. **Phased Rollout Strategy:** Rolled out the system in stages—first to head office departments, then site by site, gathering immediate feedback to fix usability friction before expanding.
